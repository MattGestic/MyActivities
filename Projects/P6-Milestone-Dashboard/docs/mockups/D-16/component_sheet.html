D-16 design standard: component sheet

Every control in the dashboard, drawn to one standard based on the Windows UI kit (Fluent 2): 24px controls on desktop and 32px on touch screens, 12px control text, 4px corners, and spacing on a 4px grid. Colours are the app's own tokens. The written spec is docs/ux/design-standard.md. Sign-off on this sheet is the gate before any app code changes.

Controls: desktop (24px) and touch (32px), light and dark

Same markup in all four frames; only the density class and the theme change. The focused field shows the keyboard focus ring.

Top filter bar, desktop 1440px (four rows)

Rows 1 and 2: Find and When as two containers side by side. Find has Activity name and Banding on one line, Activity IDs full width beneath. When has Week on one line and Show dates beneath. Row 3: Critical path on one line. Row 4: the hint and the hide control.

Total float becomes one choice set: Any, 0d, < 10d, < 3 wk, < 5 wk, Custom. 0d keeps activities with zero or negative float (the critical path). Custom opens a small panel (at or below / at or above, a number, days or weeks) and then shows as its own chip, e.g. "≤ 7d". It replaces the three separate controls plus preset buttons, with the same range of filters.

Show weeks is one field that picks a whole-week range (see the week-range picker below). It narrows the board, as the two date boxes did.

Week becomes a week picker (defaulting to this week) plus one span choice: Only, +2, +4, Up to, From. This replaces the mode dropdown and the Current week / Next 4 weeks buttons: Next 4 weeks is this week with +4. Show dates still narrows the board to a range and Full span resets it; Week still highlights, as now.

Total float: Custom

Custom opens a small panel under the chip. Apply sets the filter and relabels the chip with the value; choosing any other chip replaces it.

Show weeks: week-range picker

One field replaces the two date boxes. It picks whole weeks, because the board's columns are weeks. The panel is laid out like the board header: one row per month, one cell per week labelled with its week-ending day. It only offers weeks the board has. Click a start week, hover to preview, click an end week, Apply. The data-date week is outlined; a dot marks weeks with milestones in them.

Closed: the field states

Settings: week ending day

Sets the board's week columns, the week picker and the week filter. Lives in Settings, Defaults. Changing it re-buckets every milestone into the new weeks; comments and edits are unaffected.
